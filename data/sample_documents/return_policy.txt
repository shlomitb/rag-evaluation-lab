RETURN POLICY

1. Customers may return an item within 30 calendar days of delivery.
2. Returned items must be unused and in their original packaging.
3. A receipt or order confirmation is required for all returns.
4. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after the returned item is received and approved.
5. Items marked as final sale cannot be returned.
6. Defective items may be returned within 60 calendar days of delivery, even if the standard 30-day return window has passed. The customer must provide a description of the defect.
